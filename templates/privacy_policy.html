Privacy Policy

Your privacy matters to us. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use Car Clash 3D.
Last updated: 08 September 2026
1 Information We Collect

We collect information you provide to us, including:

  • Account information (username, email, etc.)
  • Game progress and activity (races, scores, achievements, preferences)
  • Device and technical information (browser type, session tokens)
2 How We Use Your Information

我们 use your information to:

  • Provide and improve our web application and services
  • Personalize your gameplay and track career stats
  • Maintain secure leaderboards and user authentication
3 Sharing Your Information

We do not sell your personal data. We may share data only with:

  • Trusted cloud hosting and PostgreSQL database services
  • Other players via public leaderboards and game results
4 Data Security

We implement standard web security measures to protect your information, including password hashing, secure sessions, and regular database monitoring.

5 Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access and update your profile details
  • Request deletion of your account and game data
